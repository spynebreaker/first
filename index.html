A Note From a Former Building Inspector

I had Five Seasons Windows and Doors replace all of my windows and sliding glass door in July of 2024. From the initial meeting with the sales associate, Hunter, to the measuring process with Rick, the interactions with the entire office staff, the installation process and finally a couple of minor warranty follow up issues with Dylan, I couldn't be more pleased with the entire experience I've had with the Five Seasons team.

Spending nearly my entire working life in the construction industry, 31 of which as a Building Inspector, I had the opportunity to speak with many homeowners about their replacement window projects getting both the pros and cons of many different companies as well as being able to observe first-hand the quality of the installation.

The window replacement business is an enormous industry. I can say without reservation that all window installations are not the same with some companies trying to appeal to homeowners by offering a speedy one- or two-day installation while others will take longer with a higher quality installation process, Five Seasons falling into the latter category.

From the way they measure for a replacement window or door, which there is more than one way to do that, to the flashing detail prior to installation, foaming the gaps between the new window and rough opening, to the use of high quality caulking material, along with the measures they take to protect floors and furniture during the installation and finally to cleanup, Five Seasons takes every precaution imaginable to ensure a trouble-free installation.

When I started my research, which was extensive, into which company to go with on my project I wanted to go with a local company as opposed to a national company. I heard firsthand on the numerous problems associated with national companies when it came to warranty issues. I got three quotes from local companies.

One of the deciding factors, along with several others, that ultimately led me to choose Five Seasons was an email exchange I had with the owner of a highly regarded out of state window replacement company. He was well aware of the Denver market. Although his opinion was unbiased, he had high praise for Five Seasons.

From the beginning to the end of my project, and beyond, the quality of service and quick response to any questions or issues I have had, my experience with Five Seasons has been nothing short of professional and outstanding.

— Gary H.
